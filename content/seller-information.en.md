Midnordic develops and supports Music Master, a downloadable audio application for tabletop Game Masters.

## Product and fulfillment

Music Master is sold as a one-time purchase, not a subscription. After purchasing the Full version, the customer receives an email containing a license key. The customer enters that key and their purchase email address in Music Master to activate the Full version. One license may be activated on up to three of the customer's devices.

Payments are processed by the payment provider or Merchant of Record identified during checkout. Its transaction, tax, and payment terms may also apply.
